## Shared Art Direction

- Strongly casual offline Dou Dizhu entertainment style.
- Sweet cartoon pink, warm cream, strawberry pink, coral pink, soft blue, lavender, and small pastel yellow accents.
- Use soft rounded outlines, puffy sticker-like shapes, clouds, stars, flowers, paper-fan accents, and playful card-game motifs.
- Avoid casino mood, betting mood, competitive pressure, coins, chips, ingots, cash, logos, watermarks, readable text, letters, numbers, card ranks, and readable suit marks.
